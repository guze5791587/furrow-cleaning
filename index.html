About the machine

The 1QG-1 is a tracked autonomous robot that clears and maintains drainage furrows between rapeseed beds. It navigates the field along pre-planned waypoints set on a handheld ground station, then drives the furrow and throws the excavated soil onto the bed sides, keeping the drainage channel open without compacting the crop rows.

The clip shows the operator teaching waypoints on the controller's map view, followed by the robot working in the field.
